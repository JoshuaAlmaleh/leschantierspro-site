Coverage

Where we find your job sites

We only cover cities that publish their permits as reliable open data. Here is the exact list, current as of October 17, 2026.

Covered

City of Montréal: all 19 boroughs

The City publishes its permits once a week, with about a one-week delay after issuance. Your Montréal alerts are therefore weekly, whatever the plan.

  • Ahuntsic-Cartierville
  • Anjou
  • Côte-des-Neiges–Notre-Dame-de-Grâce
  • Lachine
  • LaSalle
  • Le Plateau-Mont-Royal
  • Le Sud-Ouest
  • L'Île-Bizard–Sainte-Geneviève
  • Mercier–Hochelaga-Maisonneuve
  • Montréal-Nord
  • Outremont
  • Pierrefonds-Roxboro
  • Rivière-des-Prairies–Pointe-aux-Trembles
  • Rosemont–La Petite-Patrie
  • Saint-Laurent
  • Saint-Léonard
  • Verdun
  • Ville-Marie
  • Villeray–Saint-Michel–Parc-Extension
Covered

City of Longueuil

Data published almost daily: daily alerts (Pro and Team, at launch) mainly cover Longueuil. Includes the declared value of work when published.

  • Longueuil (Vieux-Longueuil)
  • Saint-Hubert
  • Greenfield Park
  • Le Moyne

The other cities in the Longueuil agglomeration (Boucherville, Brossard, Saint-Bruno-de-Montarville, Saint-Lambert) are not covered: they issue their own permits.

Not covered

Independent cities on the island of Montréal

These 15 municipalities issue their own permits and do not publish them as open data. They are not part of the City of Montréal.

  • Baie-D'Urfé
  • Beaconsfield
  • Côte-Saint-Luc
  • Dollard-des-Ormeaux
  • Dorval
  • Hampstead
  • Kirkland
  • L'Île-Dorval
  • Montréal-Est
  • Montréal-Ouest
  • Mont-Royal
  • Pointe-Claire
  • Sainte-Anne-de-Bellevue
  • Senneville
  • Westmount

Note: Pierrefonds-Roxboro and L'Île-Bizard–Sainte-Geneviève, in the West Island, are Montréal boroughs and are covered.

Later

Laval

Laval publishes its permits as open data, but the file hasn't been updated since March 31, 2026. We'll add it once the data is current again.

Later
