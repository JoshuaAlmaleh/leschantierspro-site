Alert fields

Exactly what the city publishes, nothing more

No owner names, phone numbers or emails are collected or shown. We do not enrich the data (Quebec Law 25).

Work addressAs published by the city. On this website, the civic number is hidden.
Borough or sectorMontréal borough or Longueuil sector, with the city.
Issue dateThe date the city issued the permit.
Permit typeThe municipal label, as is (it varies from borough to borough).
Trade(s)One permit can match several trades.
Work descriptionThe text published by the city, in French, trimmed to about 220 characters.
Declared valueLongueuil only, shown from $1,000.
Listed contractorLongueuil only, when the City publishes the business name. Not reproduced on this website.
